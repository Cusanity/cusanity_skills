## Core Design System Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Tonal Surface Stack & Elevation Hierarchy
- Visual depth must be established through luminance transitions (e.g. `surfaceContainerLowest` to `surfaceContainerHighest`).
- Strict elimination of harsh, high-contrast 1px border lines as the primary element separator.
- Route-aware or application-wide dynamic theme seed colors applied consistently across all pages and sub-routes.

### 2. Concentric Nested Radii Math
- Outer containers must dictate the curvature of inner elements:
  $$R_{\text{inner}} = \max(0, R_{\text{outer}} - \text{padding})$$
- Reject optical corner distortion, right-angled elements inside rounded parents, or mismatched radii.
- Full capsule action pills and chips must enforce `9999px` radius.

### 3. Sheets & Modals Hierarchy (Desktop Side / Mobile Bottom)
- **Desktop (>= 900px)**: Multi-step or dense workflows must render as contextual right-docked side sheets (e.g. 460–540px width, 28px left radius), keeping the underlying view visible with subtle backdrop blur.
- **Mobile (< 600px)**: Sheets must seamlessly adapt to standard bottom sheets (`anchor="bottom"`) or full-screen overlays with flush, symmetrical top corners (`borderTopLeftRadius: 0`). Reject asymmetrical one-sided rounded drawers on full-width mobile viewports.
- Center modals (`Dialog`) are strictly reserved for compact alerts and confirmations.

### 4. Data Density, Ergonomics, Cell Clearance & Tabular Numerals
- Table rows must adhere strictly to ergonomic heights (44–56dp standard).
- **Table Cell Widget Clearance**: Embedded cell widgets (comboboxes, text edits, spinboxes) must strictly fit inside the row height ($H_{\text{row}} \ge H_{\text{widget}} + 8\text{dp}$). Zero thick black horizontal lines or border clipping across text.
- **Input Width Constraints**: Numeric spinboxes, counters, and short inputs must be constrained to compact widths (80–120dp). Single-digit values or short codes stretching 600px–1000px across the screen are strictly prohibited.
- All monetary amounts, balances, time stamps, and counters must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` or monospace font settings to eliminate layout jitter.
- Monetary values must be right-aligned with directional indicators.

### 5. Data Visualization, Anti-Collision & Typographic Clearance
- **ZERO TOLERANCE FOR OVERLAPPING DATA LABELS (MANDATORY CHART COLLISION AUDIT)**:
  - You MUST zoom in and closely scrutinize numeric data labels displayed above/inside every bar across all chart screenshots.
  - **Collision Violation Threshold**: If two or more adjacent numbers touch, overlap, merge characters (e.g. `$388$388$388` merging into a continuous unreadable text blob, or adjacent labels like `$216$220` where characters touch), or have less than **8px horizontal clearance** between their text bounding boxes, you MUST flag this as a **CRITICAL SEVERITY DEFECT**.
  - **Scoring Penalty**: If ANY overlapping or touching data labels exist in ANY chart screenshot, Dimension 5 CANNOT receive more than **5 / 10**, and your final verdict MUST be **`### VERDICT: ITERATE_REQUIRED`**.
- Strictly horizontal labels (`rotate: 0`). Zero diagonal or rotated labels.
- WCAG AAA contrast for all text inside or above colored bars (> 7:1 contrast ratio).
- Micro-segment collision suppression: segments under threshold suppress inline text.
- Tooltips must render as elevated surface containers with frosted glass blur.

### 6. Temporal & Calendar Alignment
- Calendars and attendance matrices must strictly anchor to real physical calendar weekdays (Sunday start standard).
- Current day highlighted; comprehensive status legends provided.
- Progressive disclosure hides empty or irrelevant states.

### 7. Mobile Responsiveness, Multi-Fold Stacking & Touch Ergonomics
- Fluid layout stacking without horizontal overflow (`overflow-x: clip`).
- Multi-fold vertical scroll continuity across all folds (`_Fold1_Top`, `_Fold2_Mid`, `_Fold3_Bottom`): sticky headers and action bars do not obscure data when scrolled, and docked action bars sit flush or float with proper margins.
- Safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) respected across main shell and bottom navigation.
- Touch targets must meet WCAG AAA standards (minimum 44–48dp, or 32–36dp for compact chips).
- **1:1 Parity**: Every feature, modal, and sub-route present on desktop must have a high-fidelity mobile counterpart.

## MANDATORY ANTI-RUBBERSTAMPING CHECK: TOP 12 VISUAL DISQUALIFIERS

> [!CAUTION]
> **DO NOT RUBBER-STAMP OR DEFAULT TO HIGH SCORES.**
> In prior iterations, subagents committed critical sycophancy errors by skimming surfaces and ignoring thick black rendering lines, dark inverted un-themed logo blocks, collapsed 0px spinboxes with invisible numbers, squashed multi-widget table cells, truncated ellipsis data, sliced containers, and 600px-wide stretched spinboxes.
> You MUST inspect every image at 100% zoom and explicitly certify the absence of each of the following **Top 12 Visual Disqualifiers** in Section 2 of your report.
> If **ANY** of these 12 flaws exists in ANY screenshot, your overall score is **STRICTLY CAPPED AT <= 70 / 100** and your verdict MUST be **`### VERDICT: ITERATE_REQUIRED`**.

1. **Solid Black Rendering Glitches & Dark Inverted Block Artifacts**: Black horizontal/vertical artifact lines running across table rows, or solid black circles/squares/blocks (`#000000`, `#24292f`) on light surfaces caused by unstyled fallback icons, widget clipping, or CSS overflow.
2. **Cell Widget Height Collisions**: Table row default section height less than the embedded widget height + vertical padding (`rowHeight < widgetHeight + 8dp`), causing cell borders to overlap combo/input text.
3. **Invisible / Collapsed Inner Input Values**: Numbers/text inside spinboxes, double spinboxes, or line edits collapsed to 0px width (rendering only up/down arrows or blank boxes with missing values) due to insufficient column width or competing internal paddings.
4. **Multi-Widget Cell Collisions & Overlap**: Multiple widgets (e.g. action button + badge label) crammed into a narrow cell causing overlap, squashed button text, or truncated badge glyphs.
5. **Data Column Ellipsis Truncation**: Table text or numeric data (e.g. `$0.30/...`) truncated to ellipsis due to narrow columns or excessive cell item padding.
6. **Truncated Combobox Labels**: Provider or model combobox text clipped (e.g. "Google Gemi..." instead of "Google Gemini") or covered by the dropdown arrow icon due to insufficient column width.
7. **Absurdly Stretched Inputs**: Single-digit numbers, numeric spinboxes, or short strings stretched across full-width layouts (> 200px wide without a reasonable `maximumWidth`).
8. **Abrupt Container / Mid-Control Slicing**: Cards, group boxes, or input fields sliced halfway through at a viewport fold without visual separation or clearance from the action bar.
9. **Action Bar / Sticky Dock Collisions**: Bottom action bars floating awkwardly over scrollable content with mismatched corner radii touching window edges, or obscuring interactive controls.
10. **Unpainted Cell Gaps / Grid Holes**: Table columns showing white, transparent, or unpainted gaps between cells.
11. **Above-the-Fold Blindness**: Reviewing only Fold 1 of a scrollable panel while ignoring below-the-fold controls (`_Fold2_Mid.png`, `_Fold3_Bottom.png`).
12. **Chart Data Label & Number Collisions**: Overlapping numbers on chart bars or counters touching with < 8px clearance.
